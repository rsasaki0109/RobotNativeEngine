- Source: `unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_gym`

  and the 21 STL meshes it references under `resources/robots/h1/meshes`

The meshes are vendored unchanged. `h1.rne.urdf` is generated by
`examples/144_g1_sim2sim/prepare_model.py --robot h1`. The 11 links fixed to
the pelvis (torso, arms, logo and IMU frame) are merged into the pelvis, as
the upstream MJCF `resources/robots/h1/h1.xml` does. Masses add, the inertia
uses the parallel-axis theorem, and the script stops unless the result matches
the MJCF's pelvis inertial. Their visuals move onto the pelvis with composed
transforms. Only the pelvis's own collision mesh is kept on the merged pelvis,
because RNE builds one convex hull per link. Mesh paths point at `meshes/`
beside the URDF. The ten leg links, their joints, limits and collision meshes
are unchanged. `../unitree_h1_walk.rne.robot.toml` gives every link the convex
hull of its collision mesh, as MuJoCo does for mesh geoms. The links collide
with the floor only. In the upstream MuJoCo scene they also collide with each
other (except parent and child). An undisturbed walk touches the floor only
with the feet on both engines; after a hard push MuJoCo's legs can strike each
other (example 144's robustness sweep).
`../unitree_h1_walk_self_collision.rne.robot.toml` collides them as MuJoCo
does: `self_collisions = true` with `parent_child_collisions = false`. The
pretrained
policy that drives it is under `assets/policies/unitree_h1_walk`.
