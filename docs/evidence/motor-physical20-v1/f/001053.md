# New isolated ForceBased physical diagnostics reader

This is a prospective, separately registered 20-condition / 120-process study. It compares two source-bound builds, with ON, fresh ON and OFF for each case. It never launches a simulator. The earlier 12-process pilot is superseded without collection. Earlier calibration cohorts and their four primary criteria are unchanged; the previously rejected global diagonal intervention remains rejected.

The fixed plant is a contact-free X-revolute sphere of nominal radius 0.1 m, mass 0.5 or 2 kg, coincident fixed parent and identity joint frames. One outer interval is 0.5 or 2 ms, with N=1, PGS=1, stabilization=2, stiffness 300 Nm/rad, damping 12 Nm s/rad, torque cap 2 or 0.01 Nm, and targets ±0.001 rad. Four zero-target controls use cap 2 Nm. The outside-copy candidate changes only the ForceBased row diagonal from inverse(w) to inverse(w+g); it is a diagnostic, not a production change.

## Independent SI comparison

For exact nominal decimals, I=(2/5)mR² and q0=v0=0. The independent clipped backward-Euler reference is

```
v_free = (I*v0 + h*k*(target-q0)) / (I + h*d + h*h*k)
p_BE = clip(I*(v_free-v0), -h*cap, h*cap)
v_BE = v0 + p_BE/I
q_BE = q0 + h*v_BE
tau_BE = p_BE/h
```

The reader separately retains the PGS impulse before integration, the final net impulse after stabilization, geometric I times each observed deltaV, the actual integrated coordinate, the MB velocity used for integration and final writeback velocity. It never substitutes final velocity into the integrated coordinate. Native motor lambda has the opposite sign from applied physical impulse.

The predeclared limits are deltaI=max(1e-8,2e-5*abs(I)) kg m² and deltaP=max(1e-8,0.01*abs(p_BE)) Nm s. Velocity, coordinate and torque limits use deltaP/I, h*deltaP/I and deltaP/h with nominal I and h. These are **independently declared nominal diagnostic comparisons**, not conservative propagated f32/input/inertia uncertainty envelopes. Passing the impulse and inertia limits does not imply passing any other comparison. Every signed/absolute error and limit is retained. Authoritative f32 input residuals, independent pre-step public inertia and first cached row 1/(J*W) are separate. Public mass equality is an exact source-domain gate only for the two registered powers of two. Zero motion is an exact mathematical-null flag with signed raw zero words retained.

## Qualification and scope

Collection completeness, original exits, source fidelity, baseline/candidate difference and new SI diagnostic labels are distinct. Schema/identity/phase/cache/truncation faults fail closed; finite arithmetic outside the source replay domain remains excluded/unqualified. Unchanged captured words are a valid inconclusive comparison. No favorable difference is required.

The native replay uses literal source-ordered binary32 operations and an exact rational conditional row reference. Only normal/zero inputs and normal/zero intermediates qualify. Subnormal inputs, tiny nonzero exact intermediates, overflow and nonfinite values retain explicit exclusions. Actual runtime rounding mode/MXCSR/FTZ/DAZ is unmeasured. Impulse-v1 validation covers the exact single-slice sign/promotion/bound/duration projection and captured equality, not general multi-slice f64 aggregation.

Native checkpoint scope is sorted serialized individual bodies, collider, unique multibody, parameters and public words. Producer exact-consumption/reencoding is source-bound; this reader is not an independent private-object layout decoder. Full-set metadata, private pipeline/solver/contact caches and observer buffers are excluded. Captured row/public inertia checks do not prove the complete mass-matrix/LU or external-acceleration construction. Hardware, coupled-contact causality, calibration improvement, adoption and maturity are unqualified.

The frozen master invokes this reader once after all 120 original processes and the original checked coordinator/CLI exits are zero. A fixed cohort audit claim consumes the audit before reading inputs and blocks another output-path retry, including after failure. The registered literal wrapper hash, source/compiler/two-binary admission, ordered process index, durable launch claims and before/after guards are mandatory. ON/fresh completed-record bytes and typed ordered telemetry must agree; ON/fresh/OFF checkpoints must agree; OFF observers must be null.

```
  --spec ABS_ADMITTED_WRAPPER --spec-sha256 LITERAL_SHA256 \

```

Source preparation tests use invented word records, exact rational examples and injected fake processes while subprocess.Popen is blocked. They construct no native object, run no fixture and read no historical numerical observations. Retained failed pure attempts are preparation failures, not simulator results. Production adoption would still require the unchanged historical primary criteria and exact examples 133–138/heavy-grasp gates; this study cannot waive them.
