## Cap/inertia/time invariants and controls

Low-ceiling cases are intended to saturate. For the selected worst-case smallest
nominal I=.002kg m² and largest h=.002s, torque at the full .01Nm cap under the
implicit pair is 300*.001−(12*h+300*h²)*.01/I=.174Nm>.01Nm. This is an analytic
illustration of the proposed fixture, not engine data; eligibility must use actual
declared/native input words and qualification. Then independently require
p≈h*ceiling, ΔV≈p/I and Δq≈h²*ceiling/I. At fixed mass/cap, the fourfold h change
has fourfold impulse/velocity and sixteenfold coordinate scaling; at fixed h/cap,
fourfold mass has inverse-fourfold velocity/coordinate response. Qualification
and rounding must be propagated, not replaced by exact unrounded ratios.

The high-ceiling geometric references are interior for the declared inputs;
record actual boundary activation separately. Both signs must satisfy their own
reference, rather than averaging cancelling signed errors. Four zero controls
must remain motionless with all actual motor impulses zero; preserve signed-zero

object/word/parameter within each variant/case; fresh-ON ordered completed records
match literally, OFF allthree observers null. Cross-build states are measured
comparisons, not assumed exact equality.
