```sh

  --example 143_motor_construction -- \
  --target negative --observer on --label negative-on \
  --output /absolute/existing-directory/negative-on.jsonl.gz
```

are exclusive new gzip JSONL files claimed before fixture construction. ON retains
all three typed/word-preserving observers; OFF observations are null. Preserve
